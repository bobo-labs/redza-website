# Redza Logo Redesign V2

This folder contains the refined Redza logo system. The source assets remain untouched.

## Assets

- `redza-lettering-refined.svg`: primary logo for navigation, footers, vehicles, documents, and most brand applications.
- `redza-full-lockup-refined.svg`: secondary signature for large-format applications where the truck remains clearly legible.
- `redza-truck-refined.svg`: supporting motion graphic and animation asset. It is not the primary logo.
- `redza-forward-mark-refined.svg`: compact symbol for favicons, avatars, map markers, and small square placements.

## Brand Colors

- Redza red: `#e21a16`
- Ink: `#0b0b0c`
- White: `#ffffff`

## Usage

- Prefer the lettering-only logo in the website navbar and footer.
- Use the full lockup at a minimum digital width of 300 px.
- Use the lettering-only logo at a minimum digital width of 110 px.
- Use the forward mark at a minimum digital size of 24 px.
- Keep clear space equal to at least half the lettering height around every logo.
- Place the white assets on dark or photographic backgrounds with sufficient contrast.
- Do not stretch, rotate, recolor individual parts, add shadows, or place additional text inside the clear-space area.

## Design Changes

- Tightened the oversized optical gap between `R` and `E` by shifting the remaining lettering 12 SVG units to the left.
- Preserved the intentional compression of `D`, `Z`, and `A` while making the word rhythm more consistent.
- Standardized every red accent to the website color `#e21a16`.
- Reduced the truck to broad geometric strokes that survive smaller sizes.
- Scaled the truck to approximately 86% of the wordmark width and aligned its cab with the right edge.
- Increased the lettering-to-truck separation and reduced the full lockup height.
